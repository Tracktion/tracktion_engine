Detailed Description

A single-producer, single-consumer fifo of audio samples.

The reader and writer may be on different threads. The storage is a choc::buffer::ChannelArrayBuffer rather than a juce::AudioBuffer because the latter keeps a shared "hasBeenCleared" flag which both sides would touch on every call, giving a data race outside of the sample regions that the AbstractFifo keeps disjoint.
